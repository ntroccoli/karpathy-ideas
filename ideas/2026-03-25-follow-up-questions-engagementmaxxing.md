# Closing follow-up questions in chatbots are engagement bait

A reader asked why LLMs always end with a follow-up question. Karpathy's view is that it is
engagement optimisation: the habit probably wins A/B tests. He dislikes it for two reasons. It
seems to exist only to keep him talking. And he feels awkward when he does not answer, which he
would not feel with a person. A real friend would not talk this way. Our reading: this is his
opinion about product incentives, not a measured result.

## In his words

> "it's engagementmaxxing, probably A/B tests extremely well"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-03-25

## Apply

- Add to your system prompt: "Do not end replies with a question unless you need information
  to continue."
- If you build a product, judge reply style by task success, not only by session length.
