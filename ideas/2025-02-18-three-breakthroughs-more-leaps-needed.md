# Three breakthroughs so far; autonomous agents need a few more

Asked whether LLM limits come from the architecture, Karpathy first limited the question. Today's
evals are small "task" problems served neatly, even when hard. When people talk about AGI they
mean an autonomous swarm of agents doing long jobs across society. He believes that needs major
research breakthroughs and not scaling alone.

He counted three breakthroughs so far: the Transformer (about 2017), ChatGPT with supervised
finetuning and RLHF (about 2022), and reinforcement learning for thinking models (about 2024),
which is still maturing. He expects a few more of that size.

A month earlier he made the same split on the Humanity's Last Exam benchmark: every task served
on a platter will improve, even PhD-level ones, but jobs needing long, error-correcting chains
of tasks will take longer, a Moravec's paradox effect. He called the "last exam" name misleading.

## In his words

> "I think we need a few more conceptual leaps of this class."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-02-18
