# To learn something, build it yourself without copy-paste

Asked how to learn from his nanochat repo, Karpathy says to put the repo on one monitor and
rebuild it on the other. You may look. You may not copy and paste. Building forces you to meet
what you do not know that you do not know. Tiny details are misarranged and you only think you
have the knowledge. He says not to write posts or make slides in place of this: build the code,
arrange it and get it to work. He adds that a final repo hides how it was grown in chunks, so a
build-up video would help.

## In his words

> "If I can’t build it, I don’t understand it."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-10-17

## Apply

- Rebuild a small project from a reference with a rule: read, then type it yourself.
- Use an agent as tutor (ask it to explain), not as the typist, while you learn.
- Write a short test or demo that proves your version works before you compare with the original.
