# What is the best possible pretraining data?

He asked what the highest-grade pretraining stream would look like if quality were the only goal and quantity did not matter. His guess: textbook-like content in markdown, or samples from a very large model. He wants to know how strong a 1B model trained on 10B tokens of such data could be, and how far "micromodels" can go. Books already appear in data mixes, but when he looks closely they are full of bad formatting, padding, OCR errors and figure text mixed into the main text. He has never seen a stream that felt perfect. In a reply he called a leading open data mix "terrible" compared to what he can imagine, since makers trade quality for quantity.
