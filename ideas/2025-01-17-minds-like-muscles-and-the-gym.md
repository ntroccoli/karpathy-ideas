# People may train their minds the way they go to the gym

A reply to a worry that skills AI can teach, AI can also perform for the economy, so learning
loses its point. Karpathy answers with an analogy from the physical world. Machines removed the
economic need for muscle, but people still go to the gym in large numbers. In a world of high
automation, the economy will need fewer human brains. People will still do the mental
equivalent of going to the gym, for the same reasons.

Our reading: he separates the economic reason to learn from the personal one, and says only
the first one weakens.

## In his words

> "People don’t need muscles when we have machines but still go to gym"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-01-17
