# Share your beginner questions to an LLM with the author

Experts explain badly because of the curse of knowledge: they forget what is hard at the start.
Karpathy does something about this as a reader. Given a biology paper he did not follow, he put
the paper in ChatGPT's context, asked his many basic questions, and shared the thread with the
author, hoping it would help them explain better. He would like readers to send him their chats
about his own material for the same reason. Dwarkesh adds a related trick: authors are clearer
when they say the idea out loud, as at lunch, than when they write it. Karpathy asks why that is
not the abstract.

## Apply

- Read hard material with the source in context and ask the naive questions without shame.
- Send authors or colleagues the thread of questions that confused you.
- Before you publish, dictate the idea in three sentences and compare it with your draft's opening.
- Put that plain summary at the top of your docs.
