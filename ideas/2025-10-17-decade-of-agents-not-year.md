# This is the decade of agents, not the year of agents

Karpathy uses Claude and Codex daily and finds them impressive. But he says they do not yet work
like an employee or intern you would hand work to. They are not smart enough, they cannot use a
computer well, and they have no continual learning: you cannot tell them something and expect
them to remember it. He expects about a decade to fix these gaps. He bases the number on roughly
15 years of watching predictions in AI. The problems look solvable but hard. He says he was
reacting to over-prediction in the industry, and that he is optimistic about the result.

## In his words

> "In my mind, this is more accurately described as the decade of agents."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-10-17

## Apply

- Plan around today's agent as a fast but forgetful junior. Give it written context every time.
- Keep notes files, specs and checks that carry memory for the agent across sessions.
- Treat timeline claims by vendors as marketing unless you can test them on your own tasks.
