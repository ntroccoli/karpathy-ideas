# Generated video can be optimised directly

After Veo 3 added audio, he listed four points. Video is the highest-bandwidth input to the brain. It is the easiest and most fun medium, since most people dislike reading and writing. The cost to make it is near zero. And for the first time, video is directly optimisable. Until now, platforms ranked a finite pool of clips made by people, a weak optimiser. A generator is a neural net, so you can aim gradient descent, or even just prompt search, at an objective such as engagement or ad clicks. He fears what "optimal" will look like. In replies he said media will trend toward drugs, and that influencers already optimise for attention, not truth.
