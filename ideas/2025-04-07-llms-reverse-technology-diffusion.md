# LLMs reverse the usual path of technology diffusion

Electricity, computers, flight and the internet all reached the military and large firms before
they reached individuals. LLMs went the other way. ChatGPT reached hundreds of millions of
weekly users, and it costs little or nothing. Karpathy gives three reasons why firms gain less.
First, an LLM is a broad but shallow expert. A firm already has deep experts, so it only gets a
little better at what it did. A person can now do things they could not do at all. Second, firms
have complex constraints that do not fit in a context window, and a bad error costs a lot.
Third, large organisations move slowly. He warns that this may change if money can buy much
better models, and the rich could then get far more intelligence than others.

## In his words

> "Money can't buy better ChatGPT."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-04-07

## Apply

- As an individual, use LLMs for work outside your own expertise first (legal text, data
  analysis, small apps, papers). That is where the gain is largest.
- In a company, start with tasks that are small, low-risk and easy to check, not with large
  integrations.
- Watch the gap between free and paid models. If it widens, plan for unequal access.
