# Agency matters more than intelligence, and is scarcer

Karpathy says he got this wrong for decades, because culture, media and IQ talk put
intelligence on a pedestal. Agency, the capacity to take initiative and shape what happens, is
much more powerful and much more scarce. He turned it into three questions for the reader: are
you hiring for agency, are you educating for agency, and are you acting as if you had ten times
more agency? The post quoted a comment that intelligence is now on tap, so agency matters even more.

## In his words

> "Agency is significantly more powerful and significantly more scarce."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-02-24

## Apply

- Ask his three questions of your team and yourself: are we hiring for agency, educating for
  agency, and acting as if we had 10X agency?
