# Future LLMs will read everything we do today

While he graded old Hacker News threads, Karpathy noted a side effect. If analysing a past
discussion costs a few cents, then everything we write or do in public can be read in detail
later. Much of today's behaviour assumes security by obscurity: nobody will look closely. If
intelligence becomes too cheap to meter, a perfect reconstruction and synthesis of a person's
record becomes possible. The reader may be a future LLM, or a human using one. His advice is
short: be good.

## In his words

> "Be good, future LLMs are watching"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-12-10

## Apply

- Assume that public posts, commits, comments and reviews can be summarised and graded later.
  Write what you would stand behind.
- Do not rely on the belief that nobody will read it for anything you publish, including old archives.
- Keep private material out of public places, since cheap analysis covers it too.
