# Recursive self-improvement has already begun

Reacting to a benchmark that asks coding agents to re-create the speed-ups people made to nanoGPT, he restated his view on self-improvement. It will not be a thing that did not exist and then suddenly does. Any tool that speeds up developers in building the next version counts: IDEs, search, git. Most programmers now use LLM completion and diffs, in larger and larger chunks. The benchmark is useful because it is tractable. Training GPT-2 fell from 45 minutes to 3. Agents recover little of that so far. He warns that nanoGPT is about 750 lines and covers only pretraining; real code bases are 100 to 1000 times larger.

## In his words

> "Recursive self-improvement has already begun a long time ago"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-06-30
