# Use AI to learn, but test without it

Karpathy argues that you cannot detect AI use in homework. Schools should move most testing to in-class, physically monitored work. Students need to be good with AI, and also able to work without it, as with calculators. They must understand enough to gut-check wrong answers. Verification skill is the key.

## In his words

> "move the majority of testing to in class settings"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-11-24

## Apply

- When you learn with an agent, test yourself without it: write from memory, then explain back.
- Check an AI answer against your own rough estimate before you accept it.
- Keep a short list of what you can do with no AI help, and keep it current.
