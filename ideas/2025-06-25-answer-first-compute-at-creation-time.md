# Put the answer first and spend effort when you write

Karpathy praises a format that gets to the point first and then expands for those who want more.
He calls it good and under-used, and says he gives the same advice for email: open with the
specific request, then add context below. He admits he finds it hard to apply to a talk, which is
a set of prompts for the audience more than a pack of facts. His general rule is to spend more
compute, meaning more effort or model time, at creation to compress other people's time at
consumption. He calls this the respectful and kind thing to do.

## Apply

- Start every email, report and agent summary with the ask or the result, in one or two lines.
- Ask your agent to write a short answer first, then details under it.
- Use extra model passes to shorten and restructure a document before you send it.
