# Make a long from-scratch intro to your own field

After his 3.5-hour "Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT", Karpathy said one reason he made it was
to inspire others to make the same kind of video in their own areas of expertise. His example:
he read an article about superconductivity in layered graphene and wanted an expert to take him
through that area in a three-hour intro from scratch. He said many other fields would work too.

The format is a general-audience walkthrough that covers the whole pipeline of a subject and
adds mental models. He described the recording process as simple: OBS to record, a few takes
per clip, and iMovie to stitch the clips.

## In his words

> "I hope to inspire others to make equivalents in their own domains of expertise"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-02-07

## Apply

- Pick the area you know best and outline the whole path from basics to current practice, in
  the order a newcomer needs it.
- Record in short clips with a free screen recorder and redo clips that go wrong; stitch them
  in a basic editor.
- Add worked examples and say how experts think about the field, not only what the facts are.
