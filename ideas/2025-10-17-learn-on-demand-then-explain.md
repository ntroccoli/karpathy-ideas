# Learn on demand through projects, then explain it to others

Asked for study tips, Karpathy gives two. First, learn depth-first and on demand: pick a project
with a reward and learn what it needs. Mix this with breadth-first study, the "trust me, you will
need it" style of school. He likes the first more. Second, explain things to others. When you try
and fail, you find the gap in your understanding, and it forces you to fix it. He says people
should re-explain more often. He also gives advice to experts: the curse of knowledge makes your
explanation hard for beginners.

## Apply

- Choose a small project that uses the new topic, and learn from the errors it shows.
- After you learn something, write or say a plain explanation. Mark where you got stuck.
- Ask an agent to play a beginner and ask questions about your explanation.
- Alternate: one project, then one pass of the basics it exposed.
