# Expect a slopacolypse of AI content in 2026

Karpathy predicts that 2026 brings a flood of low-quality AI output across GitHub, Substack, arXiv, X, Instagram and other digital media. He also expects more a show of AI productivity, meaning shows of productivity that sit next to real gains. A week later he linked a related worry: LLMs trained on junk web text show a measurable drop in ability, and he asked whether the same holds for people. He felt he was "getting dumber" from feeds.

## In his words

> "I am bracing for 2026 as the year of the slopacolypse"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-01-26
