# Build for the capability that exists; a real tutor is a high bar

While learning Korean, Karpathy had a one-on-one tutor. Within a short talk she saw what he knew
and did not, probed his mental model, and gave material that was neither too hard nor too easy.
He felt he was the only limit on learning. No LLM does this yet, so he judged it too early to build
an AI tutor, though the time will come. He says to calibrate to what exists. For his course he
uses AI as a helper in a loop he controls: it does boring work and speeds him up, but it cannot
create the content. He says that as a consultant, much of his value was telling firms not to use AI.

## Apply

- Before adding AI to a product, test it against the best human version of the task.
- Use AI for the dull parts of content or code and keep the design decisions human.
- Tell the AI to ask probing questions about your level before it explains.
- Say "do not use AI here" when it is the honest answer.
