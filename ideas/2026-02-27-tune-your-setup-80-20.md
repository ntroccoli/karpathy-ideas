# Spend 80% of time on a setup that works, 20% exploring the next

Karpathy comments on a chart from Cursor that shows agent requests overtaking tab-completion. At each level of model capability there is a best setup, and it keeps moving: none, tab, agent, parallel agents, agent teams, and more. If you are too careful, you leave leverage unused. If you are too bold, you create chaos instead of work. His rule: spend about 80% of your time getting work done in a setup you trust, and about 20% exploring the next step, even if it does not work yet.

## In his words

> "If you're too conservative, you're leaving leverage on the table."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-02-27

## Apply

- Reserve a fixed block each week (one afternoon) to try one new agent technique on a low-risk task.
- Write down the result. Move it into your main setup only if it beat the old way.
- Re-test things that failed three months ago, because models change.
