# The third LLM interface is a persistent teammate

Karpathy calls Claude Tag the third big redesign of LLM interfaces. The first was a website you
visit. The second was an app on your computer. The third is a self-contained entity that stays
running, works asynchronously, and has the tools and context of a whole organization. It joins
the team, and you talk to it as you would to a person. He says the idea is simple, but the hard
part is the engineering that makes it work: tools, integrations, compute environments, memory and
security. In a follow-up he rejects the view that it is a chat bot with search over Slack. He says
it writes most of the code, is deeply integrated, and makes everyone feel like a manager.

## Apply

- Treat an agent as a team member: give it a channel, tool access and memory, not a one-off chat.
- Delegate work that can run while you do something else, and review the result later.
- Judge such a product by whether it works in your real tools, not by how simple a first version
  looks.
