# Go back to RSS, with a feed you control

Karpathy wants to go back to RSS because it is open and you own your content. In April 2025 he
found RSS readers too raw and proposed a better one: a feed that fades instead of an inbox that
piles up, the social features of Twitter (reply, quote, repost, like, follow), and an algorithmic
feed that an LLM builds and you can customise. In February 2026 he said he had returned to RSS and
Atom feeds. He finds more good long-form writing and less content made to provoke. His argument:
any product with engagement incentives drifts to the same trap, even if it looks different today.
He suggested a feed reader, or a vibe-coded one, and a starter list of 92 popular blogs from
Hacker News.

## In his words

> "We should bring back RSS - it's open, pervasive, hackable."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-02-01

## Apply

- Install a feed reader and subscribe to 10 to 20 blogs you trust, starting from a curated list.
- Write the ranking rules for your feed in plain words and have an LLM score each item.
- Ask an agent to build a small reader or a daily digest if you want custom behaviour.
- Treat unread items as disposable: drop them after a few days, as a feed does.
