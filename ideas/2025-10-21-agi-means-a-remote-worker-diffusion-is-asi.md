# AGI means a feature-complete remote worker; society-wide deployment is ASI

In replies of October 2025, Karpathy states the definition he has kept. AGI is an automated system that can do any economically valuable work a human can, with the "digital concession" that physical work is left out. It is a remote worker you hire instead of a person, reached by email, chat or video calls. He compares it to a build with all features and many 9s, like current self-driving. That such a system exists is different from its use across society, which takes longer (compute limits, sensors and actuators, law). Full deployment into the economy he would call ASI. He does not rate AGI by puzzle benchmarks. His 10-year estimate is a vibe; at OpenAI in 2015 he voted for 20 years.

## In his words

> "when you're fired because GPT-n is better, cheaper, takes no equity and works weekends"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-10-21
