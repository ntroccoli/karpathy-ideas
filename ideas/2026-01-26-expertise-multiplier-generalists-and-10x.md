# Expertise may matter more with agents, not less

Karpathy asks open questions about who gains from coding agents. The "10X engineer" ratio, the gap between the average and the best engineer, may grow a lot. Generalists may do better against specialists, since LLMs are strong at filling in details and weak at grand strategy. In a reply on 2026-02-25 he gave a firmer view: calling people "prompters" is a misunderstanding. Vibe coders now get somewhere, but at the top, deep technical expertise may multiply leverage even more than before.

## In his words

> "How much of society is bottlenecked by digital knowledge work?"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-01-26

## Apply

- Invest in judgement: learn to decompose tasks, spot wrong assumptions and read code fast.
- Use agents to cover areas outside your specialty, and ask an expert to review the result.
- Hire and train for oversight skills, not only typing speed.
