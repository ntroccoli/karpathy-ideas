# Move AI predictions to betting markets

Many AI predictions live in blog posts, podcasts and tweets, where nobody checks them. He wants to move them to betting markets, which he calls the best tool we have for finding truth. The hard part is the question. A good one has a concrete outcome that anyone can resolve later. Industry metrics and macroeconomics fit best. He doubts GDP works well, and he doubts evals work, because evals are incomplete and easy to game. In a reply he gave an example: the number of employees at the five biggest call-centre companies falls by 50% by a set year.

## Apply

- When you write a forecast, state it as a yes/no outcome with a number, a source and a date.
- Prefer real-world measures, such as headcount in a task that "everyone" says is easy to automate. Avoid benchmark scores as the test.
- Reject vague metrics such as "share of firms using AI" as too soft to resolve.
