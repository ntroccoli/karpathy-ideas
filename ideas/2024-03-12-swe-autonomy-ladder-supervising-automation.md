# Software engineering climbs an autonomy ladder like self-driving

In March 2024 Karpathy compared software engineering to self-driving. The steps: manual coding, autocomplete of lines, chat that writes chunks, editors that write larger diffs, and agents that coordinate the terminal, browser and editor with a human watching at a higher level. The editor and the oversight experience must change a lot: what to look at, how to nudge, how to debug. Engineering becomes supervising automation in English.

## In his words

> "supervising the automation"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2024-03-12

## Apply

- Invest in oversight: diff summaries, run logs and state views for agent work.
- Practice reviewing at a higher level: check the plan and the result before the code.
- Decide what each agent may do without asking.
