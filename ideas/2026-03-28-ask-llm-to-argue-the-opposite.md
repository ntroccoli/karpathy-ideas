# Ask the LLM to argue the opposite side

Karpathy tells a short story. He drafted a blog post and spent four hours improving the argument
with an LLM. It felt convincing. Then he asked the LLM to argue the opposite. It took the first
argument apart and convinced him that the reverse was true. His lesson: an LLM may give an opinion
when asked, but it can argue nearly any direction well. That makes it a good tool for forming your
own opinion, if you ask from several directions and stay alert to sycophancy.

## In his words

> "extremely competent in arguing almost any direction"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-03-28

## Apply

- After the LLM helps you strengthen an argument, send: "Now argue the strongest opposite
  case. Do not hold back."
- Ask for both cases in fresh sessions, so the first chat does not bias the second.
- Decide yourself. Treat the two outputs as inputs, not a verdict.
