# Get a second opinion from a stronger model on hard problems

Karpathy reports a pattern that happened three times. His coding agent struggled for an hour on a hard problem. Then GPT-5 Pro worked for about ten minutes and returned working code. When he gave the agent that solution, the agent admired it. His lesson is that people who never hand over their hardest problems miss what current models can do. Our reading: a second model from another family also gives a check that the first one cannot give itself.

## In his words

> "If you're not giving it your hardest problems you're probably missing out."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-09-05

## Apply

- After one failed hour, stop and send the full problem to a stronger or different model at high effort.
- Paste its solution back into the agent and ask it to compare the two designs.
- Try the hardest task early in a project, not only small ones.
