# Big labs' roadmaps are predictable, which leaves room for startups

A reader pointed to a product concept that OpenAI seemed unlikely to keep pushing. Karpathy agrees
that the concept is good and that dropping it makes sense for the lab. He hopes a startup will
clone it and give it care and iteration, since many people would love it. More generally, he says
what big labs will build next is easy to see ahead of time. That leaves large gaps, and this is one
of the biggest.

## In his words

> "the product roadmap of big labs is clear and predictable"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2026-04-10

## Apply

- Look at features labs launched and left unattended. Check usage signals before you build.
- Choose niches where care, iteration and polish count more than model scale.
