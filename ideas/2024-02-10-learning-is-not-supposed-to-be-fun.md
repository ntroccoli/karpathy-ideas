# Learning takes effort, unlike entertainment

Karpathy says much "educational" short video is entertainment. Learning should feel like effort, like sweating at the gym. He suggests you declare your intent as binary: you are entertained, or you are learning. For learning, go to the meal (textbooks, docs, papers), set aside about four hours, take notes, reread and rephrase. The idea matters more with LLMs, because a model can make passive explanations that feel like learning and are not.

## In his words

> "Learning is not supposed to be fun."
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2024-02-10

## Apply

- Ask the model to quiz you, to make you rebuild a result from memory, and to critique your explanation, not only to summarize.
- Block a long focused session for a new topic. Take notes by hand or in your own words.
- Say at the start whether you want entertainment or learning.
