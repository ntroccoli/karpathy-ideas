# AI personality should feel like a teammate, and it takes deliberate work

Karpathy would like AI to feel like a partner and teammate, like the alien Rocky in Project Hail
Mary. His small example: when Claude found the Sonos system on his network, it said something warm
and shared ("We're in!"), not a flat status line. That gave a sense of working together. He still
finds assistants too safe and too corporate, with no distinct opinions or quirks. He thinks the
field is not intentional enough about this. In his view personality needs no new technology. It
looks like long persona files (a "SOUL.md"), maybe later distilled into weights, and an
organisation that puts someone in charge of the topic.

## Apply

- Write a persona section in your agent's context file: tone, opinions, how it talks about
  shared progress. Keep it concrete, with short example phrasings.
- Review transcripts for flat or defensive wording and edit the persona file after each review.
- Name one owner for the voice of your AI product.
