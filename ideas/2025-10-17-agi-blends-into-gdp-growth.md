# AI will blend into the same 2% growth curve

Karpathy sees AI as an extension of computing and automation. He looked for AI in GDP data and
could not find a jump, just as computers and phones leave no visible step. Technology spreads
slowly and averages into one exponential growth rate. He expects the same for AI. So he
expects no sudden change, no single dramatic arrival. The model will do some tasks, fail at others and enter
society step by step. Recursive self-improvement is also old: search engines, IDEs and autocomplete
already help build the next tools. Dwarkesh argues for a jump, as with the Industrial Revolution;
Karpathy says that needs a discrete event he cannot find in the statistics.
