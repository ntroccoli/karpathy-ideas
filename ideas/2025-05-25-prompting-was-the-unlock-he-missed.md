# Prompting was the unlock he missed in 2015

On "timeline compression", he said that in the char-rnn days one simple claim would have changed his view fast. The claim: make a text model bigger, fine-tune it on Q&A data such as StackOverflow, and it might become a useful assistant. Instead he waited seven years for InstructGPT. He says he did not see how general text generation is. He knew you could fine-tune it for single tasks, such as translation or image captions. The meta-idea of prompting was the missing piece: one fixed set of parameters can do all the tasks if you ask in the prompt. He frames it as a conceptual blind spot, not as evidence.

## In his words

> "it's the meta of prompting that is a major conceptual unlock"
> — Andrej Karpathy, 2025-05-25

## Apply

- When you forecast, list the assumptions you hold without proof. Ask which one a single new framing could remove.
