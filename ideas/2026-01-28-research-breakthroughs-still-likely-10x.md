# New research startups can still win; 10X breakthroughs remain likely

Karpathy rejects the view that AI is too mature for a research-focused startup to beat the incumbents. People said the same when OpenAI started, and again about later challengers, and they were wrong each time. Scaling and local improvements will keep giving gains. But the gap between frontier LLMs and a mind that runs on 20 watts is large, and much progress is happening with much confusion. So he rates the chance of breakthroughs that give about 10X, not 10%, as high enough to bet on. The hard part is creating conditions where such discoveries happen. In a reply he added that AI can go beyond a brain, for example as a hive mind without the bandwidth limits of speech.

## Apply

- When you plan research, split effort between improving what works and a small number of high-variance bets.
- Look for teams with understanding of the full stack, from algorithms to kernels.

## Context

- Quote-post on the launch of Flapping Airplanes, a lab Karpathy praised. This is an opinion, not a measured claim.
