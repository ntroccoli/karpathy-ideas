# RL gives thinking models strategies you cannot write by hand

Karpathy says that RL on thinking models produces cognitive strategies: trying angles, making analogies, backtracking. People cannot hand-program these. The model may even develop an internal language that we cannot read. He notes that a Move 37 equivalent for these models, a move no human would make, has not been seen yet.

## Apply

- On hard problems, give the model room to think and set the goal and the check, not the method.
- Avoid long step-by-step recipes for reasoning models. See [success criteria, not steps](2026-01-26-success-criteria-not-steps.md).
- Test a hard task at higher effort before you rewrite the prompt.
