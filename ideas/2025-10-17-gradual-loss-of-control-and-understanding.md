# The likely risk is a gradual loss of control and understanding

Karpathy treats superintelligence as automation continued: more digital work, then physical work, done
by autonomous entities. He expects it to look strange. What makes him nervous is slow loss of
understanding, as we layer AI through everything and fewer people know how it works. He also
expects loss of control. He imagines not one entity taking over but many competing ones that
gradually become more autonomous, with some going rogue and others fighting them off. Even if each
acts for a person, society may not get the outcomes it wants. This is why he works on education.

## Apply

- Keep a person who understands each system an agent builds. Make agents write docs and tests
  for that person.
- Review how much of your own stack you could still explain or fix without the AI.
- Record decisions and designs in plain files so understanding survives staff changes.
